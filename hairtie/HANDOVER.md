# Hairtie — owner's guide

This is the guide for running the Hairtie website yourself. No code, no jargon.
If you can use Instagram, you can use this.

Your admin panel lives at **`yourwebsite.com/admin`**. There is no password — it
opens straight away.

> **One thing to know before you put the site online:** because there is no
> password, anyone who types `/admin` after your web address can change your
> products and read your orders. On your own computer that is fine. Before the
> site goes live on the internet, ask your developer to switch the password back
> on, or to put the whole site behind your hosting provider's password.

The menu on the left is grouped by how often you'll use it.

| **Orders** | Every order. Open one to confirm it, add tracking, or print an invoice. |

## 2. Adding a product

**Products → Add product.**

Fill in the first tab and press **Save & publish**. That's the minimum:

- **Product name** — what customers see.
- **SKU** — your own code for it, e.g. `HT-CLA-012`. Every product needs a
  different one. It appears on invoices and you'll need it for Amazon later.
- **Selling price** — what the customer pays (on the *Price & stock* tab).

Everything else makes the listing better, not possible:

- **Photos** — the first photo is the one on the shop grid. Drag to reorder.
  Upload straight from your phone; they're resized for you.
- **MRP** — the crossed-out price. The discount percentage is worked out for you.
- **Colours & sizes** — one row per colour, each with its own stock and photo.
  Skip this if the product only comes one way.
- **Details** — material, weight, size, care. For bags, add strap type, closure
  and compartments here; you can add any specification you like.
- **Show this product in…** — ticking *Best Sellers* puts it in that row on your
  homepage. This is how you control the homepage rows.

> **Draft or live?** A product saved as a *Draft* is invisible to customers. Use
> drafts while you're still taking photos, then publish when you're ready.

**Copying a product is faster than starting over.** On the products list, use the
⋯ menu → *Duplicate*. The copy starts as a draft.

### Changing stock quickly
On the products list, the stock number is editable in place. Type the new number,
press Enter, done. When something runs low it appears on your Dashboard.

## 3. Handling an order

1. An order arrives. It shows on your Dashboard and in **Orders** as *Pending*.
2. Open it. On the right there is one button telling you the next step —
   *Confirm this order*, then *Start packing*, then *Mark as shipped*.
3. When you ship, fill in the courier and tracking number and press
   **Save & mark shipped**. The customer immediately sees it on their order page
   and on Track Order.
4. **Download invoice** opens a printable invoice. Use your browser's *Print →
   Save as PDF* to get a PDF.

**Cancelling** puts the stock back into your inventory automatically.

**Refunds:** issue the money in your Razorpay dashboard (or in cash), then record
it on the order so your records match.

**Messaging a customer:** the *Message customer* button opens WhatsApp with their
number already filled in.

**Customers** builds itself from your orders — there are no accounts to manage.
Each person appears once, with everything they have bought.

## 4. Editing your website

**Website Editor → Homepage.**

You see your real website on the right. Click any part of it — the banner, a
product row, the Instagram grid — and its settings open on the left.

- Change words, pictures, buttons and links. **Your edits save as you type.**
- **Nothing customers see changes until you press "Publish changes".**
- Changed your mind? **Discard** puts the page back to how it was published.

On the left you can also:

- **Drag** the ⠿ handle to move a section up or down.
- **👁 Hide** a section without deleting it (useful for a seasonal banner).
- **Copy** a section to duplicate it.
- **🗑 Delete** a section.
- **Add a section** to put something new on the page — there are eighteen kinds,
  from hero banners and product rows to FAQs and WhatsApp buttons.

### Product rows
A product row doesn't need you to pick products one by one. Choose *Which
products?* — New arrivals, Best sellers, Trending, From one category, or products
you pick yourself — and it stays up to date on its own.

### Other pages
The same editor works for About, FAQ, Shipping & Returns, Privacy Policy, Terms,
Contact and Store. You can also create brand-new pages.

## 5. Changing how the site looks

**Appearance.** Pick a ready-made palette or set individual colours, choose fonts,
button shapes and page width. The preview updates as you change things.

Nothing is live until you press **Apply to my shop**. **Reset** puts everything
back to the original Hairtie look.

## 6. Discounts

**Discounts → New discount.**

- **Code** — what customers type, e.g. `DIWALI20`.
- **Type** — a percentage, or a fixed rupee amount.
- **Maximum discount** — stops "20% off" becoming huge on an expensive bag.
- **Minimum order** — only applies above this amount.
- **Applies to** — everything, or only certain categories.
- **Total uses** / **Per customer** — limits.
- **First order only** — for welcome offers.

Switch a code off instead of deleting it, so the history stays.

---

## 7. Reviews

Reviews never appear on your shop until you publish them. **Reviews** shows the
ones waiting. Press **Publish** to show it, **Hide** to keep it private, or
**Delete** to remove it for good. A product's star rating updates automatically.

---

## 8. Store details and WhatsApp

**Store Settings** is where you change:

- Shop name, logo, and the announcement strip at the very top.
- **WhatsApp number** — one field that powers every WhatsApp button on the site.
  Type it with the country code and no spaces: `919876543210`.
- Phone, email, Instagram and other social links.
- Store address, opening hours and the Google Maps link.
- Shipping charge and the free-shipping amount.
- Whether you accept Cash on Delivery and online payment.
- SEO: the title and description Google shows for your homepage.

---

## 9. Photos

**Media** holds every image. Drag photos in from your computer, or tap Upload on
your phone. Each one is resized and converted to a modern format automatically, so
you never have to think about file sizes.

Deleting an image from the library doesn't break pages already using it.

**A tip that matters:** fill in the small description box under each product
photo. It's what Google reads, and what someone using a screen reader hears.

## 10. What you manage, what needs a developer, what costs money

### You manage these — no developer, no monthly fee

Products, prices, stock, photos, categories, homepage banners and text, section
order, new pages, orders and shipping, invoices, discounts, reviews, customers,
store details, WhatsApp number, colours and fonts, and basic SEO.

### These need a developer (one-off work, only if you want them)

- **Putting a password on the admin panel** before the site goes public. Do this
  one first.
- Selling on Amazon, Flipkart or Myntra. Your product data is already stored in
  the shape those marketplaces need — the connection itself has to be built.
  **Nothing on this site currently sends anything to a marketplace.**
- Automatic order emails and SMS (right now you contact customers on WhatsApp).
- Customer accounts, if you ever want shoppers to sign in and see past orders.
- A courier integration that creates shipping labels for you.
- Moving to a proper database, if the shop grows past a few thousand products or
  several people need to edit it at the same time.
- Loyalty points, gift cards, subscriptions, multi-language, multi-currency.
- New kinds of page sections beyond the eighteen that ship with the site.

### Third-party costs — paid to them, not to your developer

| What | Roughly | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain name | ₹800–1,500 a year | e.g. hairtie.in |
| Hosting | A small server is enough — a few hundred rupees a month | Your shop keeps its data in a file on that server |
| Database | ₹0 | There isn't one. |
| Razorpay | ~2% per online transaction, no monthly fee | Only on money you actually receive |
| Google Analytics | Free | Optional |

## 11. If something looks wrong

- **A change didn't appear on the site** — in the Website Editor, did you press
  **Publish changes**? In Appearance, did you press **Apply to my shop**?
- **A product isn't showing** — check it says *Live* and not *Draft* on the
  products list, and that it has stock.
- **A coupon won't apply** — check it's switched on, hasn't expired, hasn't hit
  its usage limit, and that the order meets the minimum.
- **Online payment isn't offered at checkout** — your Razorpay keys need to be
  added by your developer once. Until then Cash on Delivery works normally.
- **Everything went back to the demo products** — your site is running somewhere
  that cannot save files (Vercel and similar). Your shop needs to be on a normal
  server, or your developer needs to connect proper storage.

---

## 12. Backing up your shop

Your entire shop — products, orders, page layouts, settings — is one file on the
server: **`.data/hairtie.json`**. Your photos are in **`public/uploads/`**.

Copy those two things somewhere safe now and then, and you have a complete
backup. If anything ever goes wrong, putting that file back restores the shop
exactly as it was.

## 13. Before you go live

- [ ] Ask your developer to put a password on `/admin`.
- [ ] Replace the demo products with real ones (or delete them).
- [ ] Replace the demo photos everywhere — homepage banner, categories, store photo.
- [ ] Put the real address, hours, phone and WhatsApp number in Store Settings.
- [ ] Check Shipping & Returns, Privacy Policy and Terms say what you want them to.
- [ ] Set your website address in Store Settings → SEO.
- [ ] Place one real test order and walk it through to *Delivered*.
- [ ] Check the site on your own phone.
- [ ] Take your first backup of `.data/hairtie.json`.
